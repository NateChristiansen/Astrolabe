## Listing copy

**Name:** Astrolabe

**Description:** Keep your working preferences, decisions, and unfinished work in your own notes repo, so agents can pick up where you left off across sessions and machines.

Astrolabe gives each piece of work a note with its current state and next steps. A local board shows what is active and what needs your attention. Its shell guard helps catch commands that could damage other work while Astrolabe is on.

Install the Astrolabe program on each machine, then install this plugin and connect your notes. Git and a supported local agent app are required. Your notes stay in your own repo; sharing them between machines requires a remote you configure.
